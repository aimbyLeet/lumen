Lumen

Remote access for small studios

Lumen is open source. Connect to your work computer from home or another office and work as if you sat at it: steady, comfortable, with sound, clipboard and files. The video goes straight between the computers, and the team has its own server.

A sharp picture

Up to 4K at 60 frames per second, 10 bit. While the picture stands still, text and interface stay crisp; in motion the stream keeps within the cap set.

Direct and encrypted

The video goes over your WireGuard or Tailscale network and never passes through the server. The stream is encrypted between the devices.

All you need to work

Keyboard and mouse, sound, a shared clipboard and files through Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V — both ways.

Up to three monitors

Real and virtual: all in one window, one at a time, or each in its own window on the client's monitors.

Your own server, access under control

The server runs in Docker on your VPS or in your local network, with a web admin panel. The administrator approves the first connection to each machine; the apps update themselves from the server.

The host is always there

The host runs as a Windows service: you can connect even before anyone signs in. If you wish, the computer will not go to sleep.

System
Windows 10 or 11, an NVIDIA graphics card
Installation
One installer; host or client is chosen in it
Network
WireGuard or Tailscale between the computers
Access
Your administrator gives you the server's address and a token

Upgrading from a version older than 0.7.0: on client computers, first remove the old Lumen (Settings → Apps), then install this one. After that, Lumen updates itself.

The installer is not digitally signed yet, so Windows may show a warning: "More info" → "Run anyway".
